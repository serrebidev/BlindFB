# Credits

BlindFB is a front end for the [`fb` command-line tool](https://github.com/hieu-lee/facebook-cli). The tool drives a dedicated browser profile that stays logged in to your Facebook account; BlindFB runs it for everything and presents the output in a keyboard-first window.

Third-party projects it uses:

- facebook-cli by hieu-lee (upstream), and the managed backend from [serrebi/facebook-cli](https://git.serrebiradio.com/serrebi/facebook-cli).
- wxDragon (wxWidgets) for the interface.
- minisign verifies every release asset before it installs.

Facebook and Messenger are Meta's. BlindFB drives them through the CLI; it is not affiliated with Meta.
